🕊️ Whispered Memories

Whispered Memories is a heartfelt digital art project dedicated to honoring and preserving the memory of loved ones. Each piece is created with care, compassion, and a deep sense of purpose — blending creativity with remembrance.

💖 What It Is

Whispered Memories offers custom digital memorial artwork — designed to celebrate the lives of those we’ve lost. Every creation tells a unique story, reflecting the love, legacy, and quiet whispers they leave behind.

🌟 Key Features
	•	🎨 Handcrafted Digital Art memorials
	•	💌 Personalized Designs based on stories, photos, or memories
	•	💝 Donation-Driven: All profits are donated to a sepsis-related charity in memory of the founder’s father, with half also donated to a charity chosen by a dear friend
	•	🌈 Gentle, Comforting Aesthetic designed to offer peace and reflection

🌱 Our Mission

Whispered Memories was born from grief, love, and the desire to do something kind in the face of loss. It’s not just an art service — it’s a space to honor, to feel, and to give back.

Every illustration is a soft tribute to someone cherished.

🖥️ Format

All artwork is delivered as high-quality digital files, perfect for printing or sharing.
